Our programs

Five areas where one targeted gift can remove a barrier for a child. In both Salt Lake City and the Twin Cities, we focus on the same needs.

Medical care

We help children access necessary medical treatment, supplies, and related care when their families face financial barriers.

What this can include:

  • Treatment and procedure costs not covered by insurance
  • Prescriptions and medical supplies
  • Travel and lodging for specialist care
  • Therapy and follow-up care

Education

We provide school supplies, technology, and educational resources so children can take full part in school.

What this can include:

  • Laptops, tablets, and internet access for schoolwork
  • Backpacks, supplies, and school clothing
  • Books and learning materials
  • Tutoring and educational programs

Food and basic needs

We provide food, nutrition assistance, and essential household supplies to families experiencing hardship.

What this can include:

  • Grocery support and nutrition assistance
  • Baby formula and diapers
  • Hygiene and household essentials
  • Seasonal and holiday needs

Housing stability

We support families with children who face housing emergencies or other barriers to safe and stable living conditions.

What this can include:

  • Emergency rent to prevent eviction
  • Security deposits and move-in costs
  • Beds and essential furniture for children
  • Urgent repairs affecting a child's safety

Mobility and accessibility

We help children obtain wheelchairs, mobility devices, and adaptive equipment that improve independence and participation.

What this can include:

  • Wheelchairs and mobility devices
  • Walkers, standers, and positioning equipment
  • Adaptive bikes and sports equipment
  • Home accessibility modifications
