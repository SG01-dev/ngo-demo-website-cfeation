Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Little Bridges Foundation respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it. [Have this draft reviewed before publishing.]

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us through our forms: your name, email address, phone number, city, and the details you share in a request for help, a message, or a newsletter signup. Donations are processed by our donation provider, [Zeffy], which handles payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your card information.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, review and fulfill requests for help, send newsletters you signed up for, process and acknowledge donations, and meet legal and tax recordkeeping requirements.

Requests for help

Information about families and children is kept confidential and shared only with staff, volunteers, or partner organizations who need it to meet a request, and only with your permission. We don't publish identifying details about children or families without written consent from a parent or guardian.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message. To see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, email littlebridgefoundation.org@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at littlebridgefoundation.org@gmail.com.
