Bringing our work to the Twin Cities

We're expanding into Minneapolis and the surrounding Twin Cities, building on our experience in Salt Lake City.

Why Minneapolis

Our goal is a sustainable local program that starts with targeted projects and community partnerships, and grows as more charitable funding becomes available.

We're building relationships with local healthcare providers, schools, community organizations, disability-service providers, housing organizations, and other groups already working with children and families.

Our first priorities

  1. Medical care for children facing financial barriers to necessary treatment
  2. Education assistance for children who need supplies, technology, or other support
  3. Food and essential needs for families experiencing financial hardship
  4. Housing stability for families with children facing temporary or emergency housing challenges
  5. Mobility and accessibility equipment for children with disabilities or physical limitations

Work with us in the Twin Cities

Are you a Twin Cities organization serving children and families? We'd like to hear from healthcare providers, schools, social workers, and community groups who can help us identify needs.
