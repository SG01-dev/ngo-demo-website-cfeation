Need help for your child or family?

If your family is facing a barrier to medical care, school, food, housing, or mobility for your child, you can ask us for help.

Who we help

Families with children who face financial barriers, living in the Salt Lake City area or the Minneapolis and St. Paul area.

How it works

  1. Send a request

    Tell us about your family and what your child needs using the form.

  2. We review it

    We may contact you for more details or documents. This usually takes [X] business days.

  3. We work to meet the need

    If we can help, we fund the need directly or connect you with a partner who can.

In an emergency, call 911. If your child needs urgent medical care or is in danger, don't wait for a reply from us.

Request help

Please don't include medical records or ID numbers here. We'll ask for anything we need later.

Sending a request doesn't guarantee assistance. Your information is kept confidential.
