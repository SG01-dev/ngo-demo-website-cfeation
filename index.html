How project-based giving works

You choose exactly what your gift supports, and you hear what it achieved.

  1. We confirm a real need

    Each project starts with a documented need, identified with families and local partners.

  2. You fund that project

    Give to a specific project, or to where the need is greatest right now.

  3. We report the outcome

    Every project is recorded by its purpose, amount funded, who it served, and what changed.
