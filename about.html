Our story

Little Bridges Foundation began in Salt Lake City with a simple observation: many children fall behind not because help doesn't exist, but because one specific cost stands in the way. A wheelchair that insurance won't cover. A laptop for school. A month's rent that keeps a family housed.

Since 2026, we've directed approximately $230,000 to 12 charitable projects for children and families in the Salt Lake City area, across medical care, education, food, housing, and mobility.

Our model is accountable by design: identify a real need, fund it directly, and document the result. Now we're bringing that model to Minneapolis and the Twin Cities.

Our mission

To remove barriers to health, education, and stability for children and families in need, so every child can grow with greater health, independence, and opportunity.

Dignity
We treat every family as capable and deserving of respect, and we protect their privacy.
Accountability
Every project is documented by its purpose, cost, who it served, and its outcome.
Partnership
We work with the healthcare providers, schools, and organizations that already know their communities.
Transparency
Donors can see where their money went and what it made possible.
