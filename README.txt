LITTLE BRIDGES FOUNDATION WEBSITE - HOW TO USE THIS FOLDER
============================================================

1. PUT IT ONLINE FOR FREE (NETLIFY)
   a. Go to https://app.netlify.com and sign up (free).
   b. Unzip this file on your computer. You'll get a folder called "site".
   c. On Netlify, choose "Add new site" > "Deploy manually".
   d. Drag the "site" folder onto the page. It goes live in seconds
      at an address like random-name.netlify.app.
   e. Site configuration > Change site name > type "littlebridges".
      Your address becomes littlebridges.netlify.app (if available).
   f. Turn on forms: Site configuration > Forms > "Enable form detection",
      then drag the folder onto the Deploys page once more.
      Submissions appear under "Forms" in Netlify. Set up email
      notifications there so each request is emailed to you.

   To update the site later: edit the files, then drag the folder onto
   the "Deploys" page again.

2. YOUR OWN DOMAIN (OPTIONAL, ABOUT $12/YEAR)
   Buy a domain (e.g. littlebridgesfoundation.org) from Namecheap,
   Cloudflare, or Netlify itself. In Netlify: Domain management >
   Add a domain, and follow the steps. HTTPS is set up for free.

3. PHOTOS
   Every picture in the "images" folder is a placeholder that says what to
   search for. Download a real photo (free: unsplash.com or pexels.com),
   rename it to EXACTLY the same filename (e.g. hero.jpg), and replace the
   old file. Landscape photos work best; team photos should be square.
   Never caption a stock photo as a real child you helped. Use your own
   photos over time, with written parental permission for any child.

4. DONATIONS (ZEFFY - FREE FOR NONPROFITS)
   a. Create an account at zeffy.com (needs your 501(c)(3) details).
   b. Create one general donation form, plus one campaign per project.
   c. Open main.js in a text editor (Notepad or TextEdit) and paste each
      link between the quotes at the top, for example:
         general: "https://www.zeffy.com/...your-form-link...",
   d. Until a link is added, those buttons send people to the Contact page.
   When donations come in, update each project's "raised" amount and the
   progress bar width in index.html and projects.html.

5. EDITING TEXT
   Open any .html file in a text editor, change the words between the tags,
   save, and re-upload. Search for "[" to find every placeholder.

6. REPLACE OR CONFIRM BEFORE SHARING THE SITE
   - Organization name "Little Bridges Foundation" (if not your real name)
   - EIN [00-0000000] and state [STATE] (about, donate, footer)
   - Founding year [YEAR] (about.html)
   - Team names and photos (about.html)
   - Email, phone, and mailing address (contact, donate, privacy pages)
   - Social media links (the "#" links in the footer)
   - Review time "[X] business days" (get-help.html)
   - The 3 open projects, their goals and raised amounts: SAMPLES
   - The 3 completed Salt Lake City examples: SAMPLES, use real ones
   - "6 families supported" figure (impact.html): confirm it's correct
   - Privacy policy: a draft; have it reviewed and fill in [DATE]
   - Confirm charitable solicitation registration in Utah and Minnesota
